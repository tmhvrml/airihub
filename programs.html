Programs & Initiatives

Six programmes, built around the same idea: give Rhode Island organisations access to things they could not afford or could not evaluate on their own.

Our programmes

AI Compute Resources

Rhode Island’s share of a near-zero-carbon AI supercomputer, open to institutions, agencies, nonprofits, and businesses.

Statewide AI Workforce & Credentialing

Micro-credentials, cohort training, and a statewide credentialing seal built with employers.

Small Business & Nonprofit AI Innovation
