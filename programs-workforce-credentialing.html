AI skills that mean something

Rhode Island’s AI Action Plan named an AI-ready workforce as the state’s single highest priority. This programme is how we get there.

A coordinated statewide set of micro-credentials, cohort training, and education pathways, built with employers and recognised across institutions.

The problem we are solving is not a shortage of AI courses. It is that no two of them mean the same thing. Today each Rhode Island institution defines its own credentials, so certificates are not comparable, learners cannot stack them, and an employer looking at a certificate has no reliable way to know what the holder can do. We are fixing that.

Three levels of training

Tier 1

AI Literacy

What AI is, how to use common AI tools, and the ethical and social implications of using them. No prerequisites. Delivered through workshops and online modules. Competencies drawn from the U.S. Department of Labor’s AI Literacy Framework. Leads to a statewide AI literacy certification.

Tier 2

Applied AI Skills

Sector-specific applications, hands-on tool use, and data interpretation. Delivered through higher education and partner programmes. Leads to stackable certificates — industry-specific and general — recognised by Rhode Island employers and carrying transferable credit toward degrees.

Tier 3

AI Leadership and Innovation

AI strategy, deployment leadership, responsible-AI governance, and advanced technical skills. Delivered by universities and selected partners for industry professionals and senior government staff. Leads to a professional AI leadership certificate.

Cohort training for public and nonprofit teams

We run three cohorts a year for state agency employees, municipal staff, and nonprofit professionals. Cohorts are hybrid, run roughly twenty to twenty-five participants, and use the AICR for hands-on computing exercises — so participants work on their own organisation’s problems, not toy examples.

Public-sector and nonprofit seats are subsidised. Private-sector and custom employer training is available at market rates, and revenue from it helps keep the public-sector seats affordable.

Pricing. Subsidised rates for public-sector and nonprofit participants are set annually. Contact us for current pricing and cohort dates.

Micro-credentials

  • AI Literacy — all sectors, all experience levels.
  • Applied AI for Public Sector Professionals.
  • AI in Healthcare Operations.
  • AI for Small Business and Nonprofit Leaders.
  • Technical AI and Machine Learning Practitioner tracks.

Every credential is developed with employer advisory panels and validated by the Statewide Advisory Board. Credentials are issued under a statewide credentialing seal, so a certificate earned at one Rhode Island institution carries the same meaning at another.

Annual convenings

Higher Education AI Convening, each October — faculty and administrators share curriculum practice, get professional development on new tools, and connect to compute resources.

K-12 Educator AI Convening, each April — classroom teachers and district leaders build a statewide community of practice around AI-ready education.
