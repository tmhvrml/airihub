AI for organisations without an IT department

Small businesses employ 51.1 percent of Rhode Island’s private workforce — a larger share than the nation as a whole.

They are also the organisations least likely to have a training budget, a data team, or the time to evaluate a crowded AI marketplace. Nonprofits face the same squeeze with even less margin.

This programme exists for them. We reserve part of Rhode Island’s compute allocation and part of our training capacity specifically for small businesses, nonprofits, and startups.

What is available

  • AI readiness assessments and adoption roadmaps — a structured look at where AI would actually help your organisation, and where it would not.
  • Sector-specific workshops in retail, healthcare, human services, and manufacturing.
  • Affordable and subsidised compute access, so cost is not what stops a good idea.
  • Referral to the right partner — the Rhode Island Small Business Development Center, Social Enterprise Greenhouse, Rhode Island Commerce, or a higher education partner — when hands-on help is what you need.
  • Competitive AI innovation micro-grants, subject to available funding, to offset compute costs and provide technical assistance.
Pilot cohort

AI Adoption Workshop Series

A three-workshop pilot cohort for Rhode Island small businesses, focused on picking one real problem and getting a working answer to it. Practical, hands-on, and free of vendor pitches.

Pilot cohort

Building AI Readiness in Nonprofits

A parallel track for nonprofit organisations, with an emphasis on transparency about how AI is used and on measuring whether it actually improved anything.
