How this is measured

Data flows from partner reporting on a quarterly cadence. The Hub compiles and quality-checks it, and the master dataset is maintained with our evaluation partners. Only aggregate, programme-level counts are published — no personal data. Individual-level information stays with delivery partners under their own policies.
