Free Google AI training for Rhode Islanders

The AI RI Hub and Google are offering Rhode Islanders no-cost access to Google’s AI and career certificate programmes.

You learn online, at your own pace, and you finish with a credential employers recognise.

Licences are limited and are awarded by the Hub. Apply below and we will let you know whether you have a seat. Licences run through 31 December 2027.

How it works

  1. Apply. Fill out a short form telling us who you are and which programme you want.
  2. Get your licence. If you qualify, we send you an access code.
  3. Learn at your own pace. Everything is online, and you set the schedule.
  4. Earn your certificate. Finish the programme and receive your Google credential.
  5. Tell us how it went. With your permission, your story may be featured to encourage others.
